How it works

A real sensor, a live desktop, one Metal fold.

Four small parts, each doing one thing. Everything runs on your Mac.

01 · Sensor

Reads the hinge

Pulls the physical lid angle straight from the MacBook's built-in lid-angle sensor — no accessibility tricks.

02 · Capture

Live desktop

Captures the screen with ScreenCaptureKit into a Metal texture, so windows and wallpaper move together in real time.

03 · Fold

Folds in Metal

Hinges the whole desktop at its bottom edge and folds it back, with a depth-of-field blur that deepens toward the top.

04 · Menu bar

Stays out of the way

Lives quietly in the menu bar. Pause, resume, or recalibrate flat to wherever your lid sits right now.
